Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

Who is responsible

This website is operated by Westlake Expeditions (Jason and Callie Westlake), Playa Chiquita, Limón, Costa Rica, in partnership with Discover Suriname Tours. Questions about this notice, or about your information, can go to jason@thejunglewhisperer.com.

What this website collects

The expedition application form asks for your name, email address, and optionally your phone number, the country or city you are based in, your experience level, how you heard about the expedition, and anything you choose to tell us about your motivation, health considerations or dietary needs. Those last fields can contain sensitive details if you choose to include them — please share only what you are comfortable sending by email.

The site does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and does not build a profile of you.

How your application reaches us

Applications are sent through Web3Forms, a hosted form service that relays your submission to Jason's mailbox. Web3Forms acts as a data processor: it passes your answers to us and does not use them for any other purpose. Nothing you type into the form is stored on this website or on this website's server.

Why we hold it, and for how long

We use your details only to answer your enquiry, discuss whether the expedition suits you, and arrange the trip if you book. We keep enquiry correspondence for up to 24 months after our last exchange, and booking records for as long as tax and travel-provider obligations require.

Who else sees it

Your details are shared with Discover Suriname Tours only when needed to arrange your expedition, and with the travel providers (air charter, lodging, community hosts) whose services you book. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to jason@thejunglewhisperer.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority; in Costa Rica, to PRODHAB.

Hosting

This is a static website. The hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested file, browser) for security and reliability, retained for a short period and not used to identify visitors.

Photographs

Photographs on this site are the property of Jason Westlake and his expedition partners, or are used under licence from Pexels and Wikimedia Commons. If you appear in a photograph and would prefer not to, contact us and we will remove it.
